About Us

The Relebogile foundation is dedicated to building empowered, resilient, and thriving communities
We believe every child and young person deserves access to oppotunities, essential resources,
and the support to reach their full potential.

The foundation started with a teenage girl, Bontle Moyo,who deeply believed in this vision
and decided to make a change in her own community. What started as her small initiative has now grown into
a movement, expanding into neighbouring communities and touching the lives of many young people.

  • to fight poverty
  • promote education
  • strengthen community engagement through sustainable initiatives

To build empowerment, resilient and thriving communities where every child
and young person has access to opportunities,essential resources and the support needed to reach their fullest potential.

  • Empowerment
  • Resilience
  • Community
  • Opportunity
